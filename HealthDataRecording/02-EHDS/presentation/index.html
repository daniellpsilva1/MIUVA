Topic 2c · Health data recording and reuse

The European Health Data Space

Before we start: in one sentence — what do you think the EHDS is?

A common set of EU rules that lets health data follow the patient for care — and be reused, under strict conditions, for research, innovation and policy.

Key idea 3 of 3 · Data quality across Europe

Same rules, very different starting points.

Guess the country.

Who pays? In Estonia’s experience, data holders carried about 80% of the work of making data available — while their main job is care.

Whose data are missing? Low-income households, homeless people, migrants, rural populations, young adults — and older people’s records are most often paper or free text. Biased data → biased policies and tools.

Our assessment

Right idea. Uncertain delivery.

Convincing

  • The report doesn’t just list problems — it proposes concrete fixes: a common data-quality framework (TEHDAS), quality labels for datasets, “enter once” forms that fit clinical work.
  • Common rules solve a real problem: today every country, even every hospital, has its own standards.
  • Purpose-based rules plus a gatekeeper is a credible answer to the trust question.

Challenging

  • Most of the extra work lands on clinicians and hospitals that are already short-staffed — and funding isn’t guaranteed.
  • The evidence is expert opinion from 2023 roundtables, not measured results.
  • A common deadline doesn’t create a common starting point.

Learning goals & discussion

What you should be able to explain.

What the EHDS is

One EU framework, two infrastructures (MyHealth@EU for care, HealthData@EU for reuse); the data stay where they are. Who benefits: citizens, professionals, researchers, policymakers, industry.

Primary versus secondary use

Purpose decides the rules: care of the person versus a question about many people. Secondary use = permit + secure environment + results only.

The role of data quality

Interoperability makes data exchangeable; quality makes them fit for the question — and it is decided at the moment of recording.
